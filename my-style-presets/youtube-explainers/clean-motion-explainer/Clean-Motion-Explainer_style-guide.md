# My style: Clean motion explainer (from Duncan Rogoff, youtube.com/watch?v=REAOFI534lk)

Built from 58 sampled frames plus auto-captions of the reference. Colours are eyeballed from frames, so treat hex values as approximate.

- Format: horizontal 16:9 for YouTube. The reference has no burned-in word-by-word captions in the frames I sampled, so captions are off unless asked for.
- Presenter: always a circle, bottom-right (about 15% of frame width, thin orange-red ring), over every graphic. Full-frame head only at the open and the close. Sometimes a rounded rectangle with a soft shadow beside a card.
- Layout: graphics own the frame, centred on a warm off-white field with lots of empty space. Keep the lower-right clear for the presenter circle. Containers are cards (dark rounded rectangles, or white cards with a thin coloured top bar) and browser mockups with a URL pill.
- Typography: titles in lowercase bold geometric sans. One accent word per title in italic serif, orange-red ("15 years", "beat map", "run it overnight"). Small monospace caps for labels ("STEP 01 OF 06", "SKILL.md"). One idea per screen, 1 to 4 words, large.
- Colour: base warm off-white (about #FBFAF8) and near-black. One loud accent, orange-red (about #DC2E0A). Secondary accents only for UI: blue for step cards, green for ticks.
- Section breaks: full-bleed orange-red slate with a halftone dot ellipse in the centre, held about 1 second, then faded out as the presenter circle ghosts back in.
- Hook (first 12 seconds): fast montage of motion-graphic frames, one change about every second, then a title card, then the presenter.
- Pace: two-speed. Quick cuts in the hook, then long holds (10 to 30 seconds) on explanations. Single ideas stay on screen about 3 to 8 seconds, demos up to 15.
- Motion: soft easing, no hard cuts between graphics. Circular iris and expanding-circle wipes, cards sliding into numbered slots, items drifting into a cluster, blur-in text. Backgrounds blur when a card takes focus. An on-screen cursor clicks icons to show a process.
- Transitions: graphic to graphic with blur plus scale or an iris wipe. Presenter to graphic: the head shrinks into the corner circle while the graphic fades in. Fades pass through white or orange.
- Progress UI: a row of numbered slots (dashed green outline) that get a tick as each step finishes.
- Sound: voice first. Not analysed from the reference yet (frames only), so no music or sound-effect rules until I check the audio.
- Assets: logo, brand colours and fonts live in `my-assets/`. Brand fonts replace the sans and serif above once added.
