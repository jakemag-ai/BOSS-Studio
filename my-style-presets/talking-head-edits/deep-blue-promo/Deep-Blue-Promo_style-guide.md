# Deep Blue Promo

Studied from a reel: 10-minute vertical promo for an AI employee course (Black Friday offer). Colours, sizes and timings are estimated from frames, not extracted from source files.

- Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Long-form vertical (3 to 10 minutes): promos, course pitches, webinars.
- Presenter: seated at a desk in a dark-blue room with blue LED strips. Three framings, **hard-cut between them** to keep it alive: wide (desk visible), mid (about 125%), tight (about 150%). Switch framing every 4 to 8 seconds, on a new sentence.
- Full-screen graphic cards:
  - **Deep-blue cards:** a radial glow (centre #2A47C9, edge #070C2E) with a faint orbit ring, holding big white text, short lists or an app screenshot.
  - **White-mint cards:** a near-white card with a mint-to-lavender corner glow for light moments (benefits, results).
  - Each card appears for one idea, 2 to 6 seconds.
- Curved horizon transition: when a card opens above the presenter, a thin cyan **arc of light** (like a planet horizon) rises across the frame, the card fills the top 55% for a beat and then takes the whole frame. The reverse closes it.
- Result card: a "Real student results" pill, a screenshot of a post in a white card with a rounded phone clip beside it, a growth tag, then a huge cyan number ("+5,000") and "new followers" under it.
- Sale moment: glowing neon words ("BLACK FRIDAY") blur in on black with a soft white halo, then sharpen. Use once.
- Captions: sparse, small, soft grey lowercase, only for key phrases ("your life"). This is a pitch, not a caption-first style.
- Structure for a pitch: promise → pain → "someone else is doing this" → the solution → one block per product or employee → proof → offer and price → guarantee → call to action.
- Pacing: slower than the short styles. A cut every 3 to 8 seconds is right for a long video.
- Sound: voice, about -14 LUFS, a soft cinematic pad that rises on the offer. New music for every video (see `my-rules.md`). A short riser into the sale moment.
- Ending: presenter alone, final line and the guarantee, then a blue card with the button text.
