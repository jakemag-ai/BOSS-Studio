# Cream Card Cuts: sound kit (candidates)

First-draft shortlist from Mixkit, chosen from track titles, genre and mood tags. **Not auditioned yet: listen and approve before the editor uses any of them.** Links go to the category listing, where each title can be searched and downloaded (no per-track links were captured).

- Music licence: [Mixkit Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree). Free for commercial use, social media and YouTube; no remixing, no claiming as your own, no registering on rights-management services (Content ID). Attribution is not required by the licence text.
- Sound-effect licence: Mixkit Sound Effects Free License (check the licence link on each sound's page).
- Do not commit the downloaded files to the repo. Keep them in local `my-sounds/` (git-ignored) and download on demand.
- Use a different track from this list on each new video, and level it about 18 dB under the voice.
