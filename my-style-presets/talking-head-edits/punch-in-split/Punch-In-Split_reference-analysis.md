# Punch-In + Split: reference analysis (Desire Hook Method reel, facebook.com/reel/1911480050212333)

100.6 s, 1080x1920, 29.97 fps, stereo 44.1 kHz. Talking-head explainer.

**How this was checked (2nd pass, 2026-11-11):** all 20 scene cuts viewed frame by frame at 12 fps
(0.3 s before to 0.4 s after each cut); the whole video viewed at 1 fps; word-level transcript;
audio measured on the stereo side channel (voice is mono, so anything in stereo is an added sound)
and every sound spike matched to the frames it lands on. Marked **[seen]** = confirmed in frames,
**[measured]** = confirmed in the audio data, **[not confirmed]** = could not be verified.

## Core structure
Two layouts, switched by hard cuts, about every 2-12 s:

1. **Split (graphic) layout**, about 70% of runtime **[seen]**
   - Black canvas with a faint dot grid.
   - Top 60%: the explainer graphic for the sentence being spoken.
   - Caption line at about 66% of the height.
   - Bottom 25%: the speaker in a rounded-corner window (wide shot, room visible). The window never
     moves or resizes for the whole video.
2. **Punch-in (face) layout** **[seen]**
   - Speaker fills the 9:16 frame: a tight chest-up shot with a heavily blurred background
     and teal/warm rim lights.
   - It is the same take as the window (gestures continue across the cut). The camera holds still
     inside the shot: no zoom, push or shake.
   - Used for personal or punchy lines: "This is called the desire hook method", "If you nail all
     three...", "this can either be...", "Either way...", "If you're selling...", "Now, the third
     part...", "anything that credentializes... yapper with low trust", "If you want to get
     better... comment".

Cut times: 4.4, 8.0, 16.6, 19.1, 25.4, 27.6, 29.1, 31.2, 33.3, 38.0, 50.7, 55.9, 64.4, 67.3, 68.9,
70.4, 78.3, 84.0, 88.5, 91.8 s. They alternate face -> split -> face.

## 1. Transitions
- **All 20 layout changes are straight hard cuts [seen]**: no dissolve, whip, zoom or flash frames.
  The new layout is fully there on the first frame.
- What makes the cuts feel smooth: the split layout always comes back already animating. Its first
  element is mid-entrance on frame 1 (sliding, rising or flickering in).
- **Graphic-to-graphic changes inside the split layout [seen]:**
  - Horizontal push: the script card exits left while a phone mockup slides in from the right (2.3-2.9 s).
  - The section tab slides in from the right edge ("Proof Anc..." clipped, then settles), at 64.4 s.
  - New cards rise from the bottom while fading in ("A Millenial Mom", the Proof card).
  - Stacked cards shift upward to make room for the next card.

- **Blur-to-sharp stagger [seen]:** list items appear one by one, blurry, then sharpen (around 12-15 s).
- **Typewriter [seen]:** "Comment 'Lead'" types letter by letter; the red "Better The Proof..." bar

- **Opacity flicker entrance [seen]:** the Video Script card blinks in (on, dim, on) at 55.9 s; the
  "Better The" bar flickers in the same way at 84 s.
- **Count-up [seen]:** a revenue number ticks $154k -> $228k beside a growing green line chart (around 2-4 s).

- **Orbit [seen]:** rings of green and red dots around the phone, with the platform icon on the

## 4. VFX
- **Red shockwave circle [seen]:** a solid red disc pops on the phone mockup, expands, and fades to a
  ring (10.0-10.8 s). It is used again on the proof phone ("true.").
- **Push-in on the phone mockup [seen]:** the phone scales up toward camera (around 8.3-9.1 s) so the
  screen is readable.
- **Arc sweep [seen]:** curved white arcs swing around the Proof card as the phone rises in (around 72 s).
- **Look:** dark glass panels (1 px light border, soft shadow) and dot-grid background. No film
  grain, light leaks, glitches or shake.
- **Speaker window:** rounded corners; the speaker's set has cyan practical lights.

## 5. Walkthroughs, explainer graphics, and zoom-ins on spoken words
- Every sentence in the split layout gets its own graphic, and it appears on the exact word **[seen]**:
  - "millennial mom" -> its card; "psychographics... trendy mom in her 40s" -> a second card.
  - "identity of the person you want to buy" -> the people grid and highlight.
  - "pain point that's in the way" -> the red node, then the line breaks.
  - "This is how you teach your kids..." -> the script card types the line, then dotted labels name its parts.
  - "own proof, a client's proof, a third-party case study" -> three profile cards stack in, one per phrase.
  - "comment lead" -> the CTA.
- **Zoom-ins [seen]:** cards and the phone scale up to fill the stage when they become the focus
  (script card around 25-31 s, phone around 8 s). The face shots do not zoom.
- **Captions [seen]:** 1-2 words at a time, white bold sans with a soft shadow, at the same spot
  (about 66% of the height) in both layouts. The title phrase "Desire Hook Method." switches to serif italic.

## 6. Sound effects
- **Whoosh/riser on big graphic moves [measured + seen].** There are 6 stereo sounds, each rising for
  about 0.5 s then cutting off sharply, laid on top of the mono voice. Each one lands on a graphic motion:

  | Peak | What it hits |
  | --- | --- |
  | 2.6 s | Script card pushes out, phone slides in |
  | 8.85 s | Phone push-in |
  | 10.9 s | Red shockwave circle |
  | 43.1 s | Line breaks at "preventing" |
  | 44.9 s | Pain-point beat finishes |
  | 72.3 s | Proof phone rises with the arc sweep |

- **No sound on the face/split cuts [measured]:** nothing in stereo at any of the 20 cuts.
- **Small pops or clicks on list items, labels and typing [not confirmed]:** a mono click would be
  hidden under the voice, so I can't confirm or rule these out.
- **Music [measured]:** no music you can pick out. There is a constant faint stereo floor (about -52 dB),
  which is at most a near-silent bed.
- **Pacing [measured]:** no gap between words longer than 0.25 s. Every pause is cut out.

## Palette and type
- Black, red accent (around #C8102E), white text, grey for inactive items. Green appears only for growth
  data, and yellow only for underline bars.
- Bold grotesk sans (Helvetica Now-like) for everything, with a high-contrast serif italic for accent words.

## Across all four references (01 this file, 02-04 in the sibling files)
- **Always:** hard cuts between the punch-in and the split; a fixed speaker window; graphics that
  appear on the spoken word; captions at a fixed height; every pause cut out.
- **Common:** title lockups with a serif-italic accent word and a colored block; blur-to-sharp
  reveals; count-ups; typewriter UI; phone mockups.
- **Seen in only some:**
  - Light paper theme alternating with the dark theme (03, 04).
  - Full-frame phone layout with no speaker window (04).
  - Text or overlays on the punch-in shot (02, 03, 04).
  - Mascot narrator (02).
  - Grey dim-out before cuts (02).
  - Hand-drawn arrow and emoji pops (04).
- **Sound:** whooshes on big moves (01), impact hits on reveals (03), click/pop clusters on
  diagram steps (04). No sound on the layout cuts themselves (01, 03, 04).

## How to rebuild it in bossstudio
- Short-form edit, tight pace (remove all pauses), word-by-word captions at about 66% height.
- Two layouts from one take: full-frame punch-in (crop + background blur) and the split layout
  (graphic stage on top, fixed rounded speaker window at the bottom). Hard cuts only.
- Each graphic is keyed to word timestamps in `raw.json`. Make sure every graphic is already moving
  on the first frame after a cut.
- Add a whoosh only on big graphic motions (pushes, zooms, the shockwave), with no sound on the
  layout cuts. Use a soft pop on list and label entrances if you like.
