# Marker Map

Studied from a reel: 33-second Reel explaining an AI content system (strategy, video, edit, post). Colours, sizes and timings are estimated from frames, not extracted from source files.

- Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Short and dense (30 to 45 seconds).
- Base: the presenter sits cross-legged in a soft-lit room, handheld feel, filling the frame. The graphics sit **over her**, never covering the face.
- Hook title (0 to 3s): a two-line serif title at the top ("My ♥ Claude / *content system*", the second line in italic), with a row of small white flow chips under it: video → edit → post, connected by thin lines.
- The marker map:
  - A hand-written red word ("strategy") appears at the top with a glow, drawn on as if written.
  - **Red lines** shoot from the word to cards around the presenter. The lines draw in 0.3 s.
  - Cards are **polaroid-style** (white border, thicker at the bottom, slight tilt), each with a blurred or example thumbnail and a small view count. They are pinned with a yellow dot.
  - Mid-video, the cards form a **red circle** that rotates around the presenter with an arrow.
- Labels: small rectangular **black label tags with white caps** ("CLAUDE EDITS IT") and a **yellow tag** ("AI AVATAR") sit under the card they describe.
- Phone frames: 9:16 rounded frames slide in over the lower half for "my video / the edited version" comparisons.
- Chunky title words: big cream display words with a brown outline ("ON TREND", "THE AI MARKETER") pop in letter by letter, centred.
- Captions: tiny lower-case white word-by-word text at about 84% height, no pill. They are secondary; the graphics carry the message.
- Pacing: a new graphic beat every 1 to 2 seconds. Cards stack up as the voice lists steps, then clear for the next idea.
- Ending: a cream **event card** ("free live webinar, date and time") with stacked polaroids behind, and a small caption above: Comment "KEYWORD" below.
- Sound: voice, about -14 LUFS, a gentle upbeat bed, tiny paper-flick sounds on cards and a marker scratch on the red word. New music for every video (see `my-rules.md`).
