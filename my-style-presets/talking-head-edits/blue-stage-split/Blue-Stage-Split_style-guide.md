# Blue Stage Split

Studied from a reel: 50-second Reel listing 30 viral Claude plugins in five groups. Colours, sizes and timings are estimated from frames, not extracted from source files.

- Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps.
- The screen is **split 50/50**: the top half is the stage (graphics), the bottom half is the presenter. A caption pill sits **on the seam**, centred.
- Full-screen graphic beats (no presenter) alternate with split beats, so the presenter appears in about half of the shots.
- The stage: a **deep-blue radial gradient** (light blue glow at the top left fading to #0A2260) with faint thin circle and grid lines. A slow drift of the glow keeps it alive.
- Title card: a dark rounded card (#0C1330) with a **big gold serif number** and a white bold label ("1  Claude plugins"). Behind it, a blurred grid of small tool chips (logo, name, star count) scrolls past.
- List structure: one **group per category** (5 groups of about 6 names). For each group, name the group on the stage, then show the item names as captions, one at a time.
- Item cards on the stage (seen in the full frame set): a dark glass card with a coloured **category tag** on top (CODE blue, RESEARCH purple, TOKENS yellow, DESIGN pink, WRITING & VIDEO green), a **screenshot of the item's page**, the item name in bold, a one-line description, a **star count badge** (for example 314k), and a row of small round **avatars** under the card for the other items in the group. Cards slide up from the bottom of the stage and replace each other.
- Opening counter: a card with a big gold number ("24", then "30") and the label "Claude plugins", with a small "most viral on GitHub" line under it.
- Big words: a single large word (for example "something BIG") can sit on the stage with the key word in gold and the rest white; it appears blurred, then sharpens.
- Captions: **word-by-word**, in a dark rounded pill (#111 at 85%) with white text in a techno or geometric font (Orbitron-like), about 26px, centred on the split line. Item names stay on screen a little longer than normal words.
- Presenter: the bottom half is a desk-and-chair framing, head and shoulders, a little off-centre. Cuts between presenter and stage are **hard cuts**, never fades.
- Transitions: a quick blur dissolve (0.15 s) when the stage changes from split to full-screen.
- Pacing: a new beat every 1.5 to 3 seconds. The name list should be read fast; do not let any single name stay on screen over 1.5 seconds.
- Sound: voice, about -14 LUFS, a light electronic music bed, soft ticks on each list item. New music for every video (see `my-rules.md`).
- Ending: presenter on the bottom half with the call to action in the caption pill ("Comment PLUGINS and ...").
