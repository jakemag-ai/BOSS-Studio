# My style: Bold and energetic

- Format: vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.
- Captions: word by word. Key words in BIG bold caps, small filler words in lowercase. Exact spoken words, Taglish as spoken.
- Caption font: Anton (bold, condensed). Brand fonts in `my-assets/fonts/` replace it once added.
- Caption colors: white with a soft shadow; the key word pops in the brand highlight color (placeholder: #FFBE0B yellow, until brand colors are added in `my-assets/brand.md`).
- Caption position: upper third, clear of the app buttons at the bottom.
- Hook: one short, punchy bold line in the first 3 seconds, with a quick pop-in. Captions start only after the hook is gone.
- Pace: tight. Cut every breath, dead air, false start and retake; keep a very short beat at each join.
- Graphics: some motion. Fast zoom-ins on key words, a punch-in on the hook, simple pill labels for key terms. Logo bug in a corner when a logo is supplied.
- Color: natural with a slight pop in contrast and saturation.
- Sound: voice first. Light whoosh and hit effects on cuts, zooms and the hook, taken from `my-sounds/`. Quiet music bed from `my-sounds/music/` under the voice (about -22 dB), ducked while speaking.
- Assets: logo, brand colors and fonts live in `my-assets/` (see `my-assets/README.md`).
