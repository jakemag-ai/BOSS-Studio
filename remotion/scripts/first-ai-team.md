# How I'd Build My First AI Team If I Started Over Today

Format: vertical 9:16 Reel. Target 2 minutes (about 300 words at a natural pace of 150 words a minute).
Voice: Krista's own, direct and plain-spoken. Written to be read out loud, not on a page.
Status: DRAFT 2, waiting for review. No voiceover or video rendered yet.

## Script (draft 2, about 305 words, about 2:00)

**[HOOK] 0:00 to 0:12**
I run sixteen AI managers and a hundred and forty-five AI team members.
And if I started over today, I would never start there.
Here's exactly where I'd start instead.

**[THE POINT] 0:12 to 0:24**
You don't need a hundred and forty-five. You need one.
Just one. One AI doing one job really well.
So here are my five steps, in order. They work for any business.

**[STEP 1: teach it your business] 0:24 to 0:46**
Step one. Before you hire anything, teach it your business.
Your voice. What you sell. Who you serve. Save it all in files.
For example, I have a voice rules file. It says I'm direct. I say the thing, no throat-clearing.
And I'm plain-spoken, so a seventh grader can read it.
Skip this step, and everything your AI writes sounds generic.

**[STEP 2: pick the job] 0:46 to 1:02**
Step two. Hire for the job you repeat.
Look at everything you did by hand this week.
Now pick the one task you do over and over again.
That's your first hire. Not the biggest job. The most repeated one.

**[STEP 3: job description] 1:02 to 1:22**
Step three. Write it a job description, just like you would for a person.
What it does. What good looks like. And what it never does without you.
Mine is a daily briefing. Every morning it gives me my calendar, my inbox highlights, my pipeline, and one top-priority next move.
That's the whole job. Clear, and simple.

**[STEP 4: correct it] 1:22 to 1:42**
Step four. It will get things wrong. So correct it like a new hire.
Tell it plainly what's wrong. Then have it work out why it happened, and fix the cause.
I even have a job for this. It's called Correction Postmortem.
Every time I correct my AI, it finds the root cause, logs it, and applies the fix. So it doesn't happen twice.

**[STEP 5: hire the next one] 1:42 to 1:54**
Step five. Then, and only then, hire the next one.
When one job runs without you, add another.
Once you have a few, group them by role. Content. Leads. Follow-up. Each group gets a manager.
That's how you end up with sixteen.

**[CTA] 1:54 to 2:05**
Your team starts with one hire. So pick your first job tonight.
Comment TEAM, and I'll send you the five steps and the prompts to set up your first one.

Note: the timings add up to about 2:05 including pauses. If the voiceover runs long, I tighten step 3 and step 4 first.
