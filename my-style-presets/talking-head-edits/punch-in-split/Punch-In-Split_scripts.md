# Punch-In Split (formerly BOSS Editing - Vertical01) — Original Scripts
Topic: how to write a hook that makes people buy (not just watch).
Our own framework (new names, new examples): **The Buyer Hook = WHO + WIN + WHY-TRUST**
1. **WHO** — name the exact person in the first line.
2. **WIN** — promise the result they already want (or the pain they want gone).
3. **WHY-TRUST** — one fast receipt so they believe you.

Placeholders: [BRAND] = Free247, [NICHE EXAMPLE], [YOUR PROOF]. Replace with real facts. Don't invent numbers.
Layout key: **A** = full-screen punch-in on the face. **B** = split (graphic on top, caption, face card at bottom). Speaking pace ~150 wpm.

## Caption and emphasis rules (all scripts)
- Word-by-word captions. Switch to the italic serif on the key phrase of each beat (e.g. "their own name", "one receipt").
- Hard punch-in cut every 2–4 s of speech. No graphic stays up more than ~12 s.
- Each pill (WHO / WIN / WHY-TRUST) lights on its own line and dims when it's done, so viewers always know where they are.

## Production notes
- All examples (gym owners, bakery, freelance editors) are our own. Swap in Free247's real audience.
- Keep any numbers real. The "10,000 views / 0 sales" line in Script 1 is a scenario, so say "imagine" or swap in your actual figure.
- Record three takes per line: neutral, energetic, and a confident-quiet one for the punch-in beats.
