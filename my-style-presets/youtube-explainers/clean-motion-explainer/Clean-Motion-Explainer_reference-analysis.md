# Style guide: "I taught Claude how to edit videos and it's INSANE" (Duncan Rogoff, 9:05)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=REAOFI534lk
Evidence: 58 sampled frames plus auto-captions. The captions came back in Arabic, so I read the speech from that and the on-screen English text from the frames. Times are approximate (frame sampling, not frame-accurate).

## Layout
- **Presenter is a persistent circle, bottom-right** (about 15% of frame width, thin red/orange ring) over every graphic. Full-frame head only at the open and the close (about 0:21, 7:24). Mid-video the head sometimes sits in a rounded rectangle with a soft shadow beside a card.
- **Graphics own the whole frame.** Content sits centred on a light field with generous empty space; the lower quarter is kept clear for the circle.
- **Containers are cards**: dark rounded rectangles (SKILL.md "video-editor") or white cards with a thin coloured top bar. Browser mockups use a URL pill (prompt-motion.com).
- **Progress UI**: a row of 6 numbered slots (dashed green outline). Each one gets a tick as its step finishes.
- **Section break**: full-bleed saturated orange-red with a halftone dot ellipse in the centre (seen about 2:16, 2:49, 6:42).

## Typography
- **Titles**: lowercase, bold geometric sans ("scripting", "video-editor", "four").
- **Accent word**: italic serif in orange or red, paired with the sans ("15 years", "beat map", "run it overnight", "every step", "the whole video").
- **Labels**: small monospace caps for metadata ("STEP 01 OF 06", "SKILL.md", "01 · SCRIPTING").
- One idea per screen. Text is short: 1 to 4 words, or one short line. Large relative to frame.

## Colour
- Base: warm off-white. Dark: near-black cards and black fills.
- One loud accent: orange-red. Secondary UI accents: blue (step cards), green (checks).

## Motion
- **Pace is two-speed.** A fast montage at the hook (scene changes about 0:13 to 0:17, roughly one per second or faster), then long holds (10 to 30 seconds) while the explanation runs.
- **Reveals**: circular iris and expanding-circle wipes, cards sliding or flying into a slot, items drifting into a cluster, a blur-in on text (e.g. "four" resolving from blur).
- **Cursor choreography**: an on-screen cursor clicks icons (cut / audio / captions) to show "one prompt runs every step".
- **Soft easing, no hard cuts** between graphics. Fades go through white or orange. Background elements blur when a card takes focus.

## Transitions
- Graphic to graphic: blur plus scale, or iris wipe.
- Head to graphic: the head shrinks into the corner circle while the graphic fades in.
- Section change: cut or fade to the orange halftone slate, hold about 1 second, then fade out with the head circle ghosting back in.
