# Liquid Orange Flow

Studied from a reel: 50-second Reel about a free open-source AI video studio (OpenMontage). About 27 scenes in 50 seconds. Colours, sizes and timings are estimated from frames, not extracted from source files.

- Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Keep the top 8% and bottom 12% clear of key content.
- Base: a **black background**. The presenter fills the frame with a slight zoom, in a dark room with a neon-blue LED accent, holding a small prop in hand.
- Pacing: **a new scene every 1 to 3 seconds** (27 scenes in 50 seconds). Never hold the presenter for more than about 4 seconds without a cut or a graphic.
- Hook (first 4 seconds): a small logo icon on black, then the presenter. The first line states the promise ("this free tool turns Claude into a full production studio").
- Signature transition, the **liquid orange wipe**:
  - A flood of molten orange (a vertical gradient from #FFA032 to #F2541B) pours in from the top or the side with a wobbly, organic edge, covers the frame and then drains, revealing the next scene.
  - It runs about 0.5 to 0.8 seconds and is used between presenter and graphic, and also **between graphic beats** (up to five wipes in one 3-second burst).
  - Never a plain fade or slide.
- Graphic scenes (on black, between wipes):
  - A GitHub page or app window, small and centred, dark UI.
  - A row of cream **icon cards** (SCRIPT, VISUALS, B-ROLL, MUSIC) that appear one by one, each on its spoken word.
  - A mascot or logo with its name under it.
  - An "AI RESEARCHER" style card with a small icon.
  - Cards sit in the middle band of the frame, never touching the edges.
- Captions: **one word at a time**, centred at about 78% down the frame, in a **dark brown rounded pill** (#3A2F25 at about 85%) with white Inter 600 text around 22px. Words change on every spoken word. During graphics the pill stays in the same spot.
- Emphasis word: one key word per Reel ("FREE") jumps to the top of the frame, white, bold, heavy black outline, with a small scale pop.
- Motion: slight push-in on the presenter (100% to 106% over the shot), cards pop in with a soft overshoot, no bounce on the pill.
- Sound: voice, clean and loud (about -14 LUFS), with an energetic music bed and a **soft swoosh on each orange wipe** (the one place a swoosh is allowed). Pull new music for each video (see `my-rules.md`).
- Ending: back to the presenter full-frame for the call to action ("comment ... below"), no outro card.
