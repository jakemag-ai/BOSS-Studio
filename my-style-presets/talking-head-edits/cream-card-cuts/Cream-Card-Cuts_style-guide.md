# Cream Card Cuts

Studied from a reel: 94-second teaching Reel on writing hooks (context, pull, whiplash). Colours, sizes and timings are estimated from frames, not extracted from source files.

- Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps. This is a longer teaching style (60 to 120 seconds).
- Look: the presenter is filmed **low-key and cinematic**: dark teal room, one warm practical lamp, a rim of green light. Locked-off, medium-wide framing, seated, hand gestures. Colour grade: deep teal shadows, warm skin.
- Structure: three or four numbered steps. Each step opens with a short statement on the presenter, then cuts to an **example card screen**, then back.
- Example screens:
  - **Cream screen** (#EADFC9) or a **white flash** screen holds the example.
  - The example is shown as one or two **9:16 phone-shaped cards** (rounded 14px corners, soft drop shadow), side by side with a small gap, centred. They show B-roll or a clip that demonstrates the point.
  - A small lower-case word caption sits above the card as it plays.
  - The example screen lasts 2 to 4 seconds, then a hard cut back to the presenter.
- Transitions: **white flash** (2 to 4 frames) between presenter and example. No slides, no whooshes.
- Captions: bold white sans on the presenter, **1 to 2 words at a time**, centred at chest height, with a soft dark shadow so they read over the dark room. Words land on the exact spoken word. No pills, no backgrounds.
- Punch-ins: hard cut between two framings (100% and 118%) on key lines.
- Pacing: presenter shots 3 to 6 seconds, example screens 2 to 4 seconds, with a white flash between.
- Sound: voice, about -14 LUFS, a low ambient music bed that ducks under speech. Fresh music for every video (see `my-rules.md`).
- Ending: presenter, calling the viewer to comment for the longer version. No outro card.
